## Results

During an approximately four-month internship, delivered a **bidirectional MQTT test simulator** and developed a **ROS 2 relay between web and CAN interfaces, tested and deployed in the field**.

## My contribution

- Built a Python/Tkinter simulator with connection status, message logs, and bidirectional testing to identify basic communication issues before field debugging.
- Developed a **ROS 2 message-processing and forwarding node** and participated in vehicle tests.
- Adapted MQTT software for different vehicle models and traced defects through code and logs.

## Engineering lessons

Clear interfaces, shared knowledge of field conditions, and reproducible logs make both debugging and collaboration more effective.
